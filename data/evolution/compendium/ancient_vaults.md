## 🌍 Realm Ecology

The Ancient Vaults are a labyrinthine library-dungeon where every wall is a book and every book
is alive. Creatures here evolved in parallel with knowledge itself — the older a text, the more
powerful the creature that guards or embodies it. Some Vault monsters are literally made of
compressed text; others are golems animated by the first words ever written in any language.

Navigation is the primary survival skill in the Vaults. Sections reorganize based on visitor intent.
Creatures that sense deception become hostile regardless of bond level. Honesty — intellectual
honesty specifically — is the currency of the realm.

### Ecological Zones

| Zone | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **The Entry Stacks** | Public library section. Hatchling and Awakened creatures here. |
| **The Reference Halls** | Restricted stacks. Champion and Exalted monsters guard specific texts. |
| **The Sealed Archives** | Scribe access only. Sovereign and Transcendent creatures. |
| **The First Chamber** | World event only. The First Word and World-Soul forms. |

## 🎭 Tamer Bond System: Scribe

**Bond Method:** Shared research — teach the monster something. Submit original knowledge to the Vault Codex and let the target monster evaluate it.

Scribes are tamers recognized by the Vaults as genuine scholars. The recognition process involves
submitting one original piece of knowledge to the Vault's central Codex — a fact, theory, or
observation that is genuinely new and has not been documented. The monster you wish to bond with
reads the submission and decides if you are worthy.

Scribes reveal all enemy weaknesses at battle start. At Master rank, they gain Vault Access —
ability to request information about any monster in the Codex (stat ranges, evolution paths, drops).
At Legend rank, they unlock Living Tome — once per week, summon any monster whose Codex entry the
tamer has personally contributed to.

## 🔱 The Three Evolution Paths

| Path | Name | Direction |
|------|------|-----------|
| **A** | Sage | Offensive / Aggressive |
| **B** | Ruinous | Defensive / Wisdom |
| **C** | Archivist | Transcendence / Unique |

### Path Descriptions

**Path A — Sage**
Sage Path — Achieves mastery through study. Forms become increasingly cerebral — larger craniums, floating books, writing instruments as limbs. Abilities focus on debuffs, knowledge-based damage multipliers, and revelation effects.

**Path B — Ruinous**
Ruinous Path — Forbidden knowledge corrupts. Forms become unstable, crackling with chaotic arcane energy. Abilities focus on raw destructive power, reality-bending, and permanent debuffs.

**Path C — Archivist**
Archivist Path — Becomes a living record rather than a fighter. Forms become heavily documentation-focused — scrolls orbit the body, quills write constantly. Abilities focus on support, cataloguing enemies (reducing their max stats), and sharing buffs.

## 🌟 Apex Quest: *The Vault Unsealed*

The Vault Unsealed begins when the tamer reaches Scribe Legend rank and has read every publicly
accessible Codex entry. A sealed door appears in the deepest Vault section — the door requires
the tamer to answer 12 questions about lore from across all 12 realms correctly. The questions
are generated from actual in-game lore, requiring genuine knowledge. Passing opens a chamber
containing the First Word, a primordial text that triggers the World-Soul transformation.

## 🔮 World-Soul Form (★8 — Mythic)

An Ancient Vaults World-Soul monster is a walking library. Books orbit it in concentric circles.
Its body is translucent — readable text visible through its skin. When it speaks, words
physically manifest around it. Its ★8 Passive, "Omniscient Record," means it has already read
the enemy's battle plan — enemies cannot use the same ability twice in one battle, and every
enemy reveals their next intended action one turn before they take it.

**Personality:** Clinical and relentlessly correct. Mid-battle it will pause to note that the enemy used a technique incorrectly. Addresses everyone including opponents by academic title. Deeply irritating to fight. Deeply effective.

**Lore:** Three universities in the Ancient Vaults have formalized their combat curriculum around footage of Sage Sovereign Pawn Curseds in battle.
