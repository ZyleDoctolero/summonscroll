## 🌍 Realm Ecology

The Divine Threshold exists at the liminal edge between mortal plane and the celestial. Marble
colonnades stretch into golden clouds. Light itself is understood here as prayer made visible —
every beam of sunlight is an act of devotion by the realm to something higher.

The hierarchy is explicit and visible. Creatures are marked by the number of halos they bear, the
color of their wings, and the weight of their censers. A newcomer can read a monster's rank within
seconds. This makes the Divine Threshold the most orderly realm — and the most politically complex.
Tamers who rise through the ranks gain access to restricted temples that contain secrets about
every other realm.

## 🎭 Tamer Bond System: Prelate

**Bond Method:** Shared prayer + ritual fasting. Complete 3 selfless acts (no-reward quests) while the target monster accompanies you.

Prelates are tamers recognized by the Divine Threshold's celestial hierarchy as worthy servants.
They earn their title through 30 days of ritual discipline — dawn prayers, fasting on holy days,
and completing three acts of genuine sacrifice (in-game: completing three quests without reward).

Prelates grant passive HP regen to all party monsters. At Master rank, they can enter Restricted
Temples. At Legend rank, they unlock Divine Mandate — once per battle, all enemies must spend
their first action attempting to flee unless they resist.

## 🔱 The Three Evolution Paths

| Path | Name | Direction |
|------|------|-----------|
| **A** | Radiant | Offensive / Aggressive |
| **B** | Fallen | Defensive / Wisdom |
| **C** | Hierophant | Transcendence / Unique |

### Path Descriptions

**Path A — Radiant**
Radiant Path — Ascends toward pure celestial form. Body becomes light-dense, wings grow multiple tiers. Abilities focus on burst healing, holy damage, and purification effects.

**Path B — Fallen**
Fallen Path — Chooses forbidden power. Wings become dark. Halo inverts. Abilities focus on divine corruption, fear, and the use of holy power for profane ends — high damage, no healing.

**Path C — Hierophant**
Hierophant Path — Becomes a living institution. Forms become imposing, ceremonial, ancient. Abilities focus on auras, commandments (passive battlefield rules), and passive damage from the realm itself.

## 🌟 Apex Quest: *The Final Threshold*

The Final Threshold is the most morally demanding quest in the game. The tamer must choose
between saving a village of mortals or preventing a celestial ritual that would permanently
seal one of the other 11 realms. There is no correct answer — both choices have permanent
server-wide consequences. Whichever choice the tamer makes, their primary Divine ally
transforms into its World-Soul form, shaped by that decision.

---

## 🔮 World-Soul Form (★8 — Mythic)

A Divine Threshold World-Soul monster is described as "a sunrise given permission to be angry."
Its halo is so bright that it creates shadow — the absence of its light visible on everything
nearby. Its body is partially light and partially something older than light. Its ★8 Passive,
"Heaven's Law," designates one rule at the start of each battle (e.g., "no fleeing," "no items,"
"heal effects are doubled") that applies to all combatants including enemies.

**Unlock Conditions:**
1. Complete *The Final Threshold* story quest
2. Reach Prelate Legend rank
3. Bond of 5,000+ with the target monster
4. Obtain: **Divine Absolute (World Quest Reward — requires Prelate Legend rank)**
5. Participate in the server-wide World Event for Divine Threshold

#### 🔱 RADIANT PATH

*Radiant Path — Ascends toward pure celestial form. Body becomes light-dense, wings grow multiple tiers. Abilities focus on burst healing, holy damage, and purification effects.*

##### ★★★★★ Lightning Dark Angel — Radiant Sovereign *(Sovereign — Elite)*
**Appearance:** The dark energy that defined the Lightning Dark Angel has been pulled toward pure light — and the result is not a simple transformation but a war between what it was and what the Radiant path is making it. The wings are now two-tiered, the lower dark and the upper brilliant white. Lightning crackles holy-gold where it once crackled dark-violet. The eyes hold both colors simultaneously and will not resolve into either.

**Personality:** Has chosen ascension and is at peace with the cost of it — which is significant. The dark edge is still present, but the Radiant path channels it into drive rather than destruction. Fights with a focus that feels divine in the original sense: dedicated, absolute, serving something larger than individual outcome.

**Lore:** Radiant Sovereign Lightning Dark Angels are among the rarest attacker-class entities in Divine Threshold, because the path requires the angel to redirect its aggression from destruction toward purpose. Most Lightning Dark Angels find this impossible. Those that achieve it become the most precisely devastating attackers in the realm — their holy lightning does not just damage, it judges.

**Abilities:** Feral Strike · Devastate · Apex Feral Strike

**Passive:** *Holy Judgment — this creature's attacks deal an additional 20% holy damage against targets with active buffs; the holy damage bypasses all elemental resistances; against debuffed targets, attacks instead deal +30% base damage*

**To evolve:** Bond 700 · 20× Censer Ash, 10× Censer Ash · *Complete 5 Radiant Path missions*

##### ★★★★★★ Lightning Dark Angel Incarnate *(Transcendent — EX)*
**Appearance:** The war between dark and light has resolved — in the Radiant direction, but only barely. The form is now luminous, wings fully multi-tiered and brilliant, lightning a pure holy-white. The Incarnate form is what the Lightning Dark Angel chose, and the choice is visible in every line of it.

**Personality:** Has arrived at something that functions like serenity but came from a very different direction than serenity usually does. Has accepted what the Radiant path required of it and now operates from a place of genuine conviction. The violence, when it arrives, has a quality of inevitability — this was always what was going to happen.

**Lore:** Transcendent Radiant Lightning Dark Angels are called Incarnates because the tamer community believes they have incarnated something beyond themselves — a divine purpose that was waiting for a carrier. The battles they fight leave the battlefield cleaner than they found it, in ways that are hard to quantify.

**Abilities:** Bone Crush · Predator's Mark · Apex Bone Crush

#### 🔱 FALLEN PATH

*Fallen Path — Chooses forbidden power. Wings become dark. Halo inverts. Abilities focus on divine corruption, fear, and the use of holy power for profane ends — high damage, no healing.*

##### ★★★★★ Lightning Dark Angel — Fallen Sovereign *(Sovereign — Elite)*
**Appearance:** The halo has inverted. Where it once rose above the head, it now circles the neck like a noose of inverted light — not dark light, but light that functions as darkness, absorbing rather than emitting. The wings have gone fully dark, deeper than the original dark-angel aesthetic, and the lightning is now a bruised violet-black that leaves scorch marks that don't heal cleanly. The form is still beautiful. That's what makes it unsettling.

**Personality:** Has chosen forbidden power and found it enormously satisfying. Is not conflicted about this. The Fallen path doesn't corrupt so much as clarify: it removes whatever hesitation the Lightning Dark Angel had about using divine power without divine sanction. What remains is extremely direct, extremely powerful, and extremely honest about what it is.

**Lore:** Fallen Sovereign Lightning Dark Angels are feared across Divine Threshold not because they're unpredictable — they're very predictable — but because what they predict doing, they do. The forbidden lightning they wield was holy once. It still has the penetrating quality of holy power, still finds the weaknesses that divine energy finds. It's just no longer being used for the purposes divinity intended.

**Abilities:** Feral Strike · Devastate · Apex Feral Strike

**Passive:** *Forbidden Lightning — this creature's attacks apply a Profane Mark to targets; marked targets cannot be healed for 2 turns; if a marked target dies, this creature gains +15% ATK for the remainder of the battle; Profane Marks stack up to 3 times*

**Appearance:** The inverted halo has intensified into a ring of light-that-consumes rather than emits. The wings are now an architecture of dark divine energy, structured and deliberate rather than chaotically dark. **Lore:** Only a few Transcendent Fallen Lightning Dark Angels exist in the Divine Threshold records, and the records about them have the quality of warning documents. #### 🔱 HIEROPHANT PATH

*Hierophant Path — Becomes a living institution. Forms become imposing, ceremonial, ancient. Abilities focus on auras, commandments (passive battlefield rules), and passive damage from the realm itself.*

##### ★★★★★ Lightning Dark Angel — Hierophant Sovereign *(Sovereign — Elite)*
**Appearance:** The lightning dark angel aesthetic has been formalized — robes have appeared around the form, not replacing the wings or the lightning but contextualizing them as aspects of an office rather than natural features of a creature. The dark-and-light contrast has become heraldic: organized into patterns that communicate rank, history, and institutional authority. The ceremonial weapons it carries are made of the same divine lightning it always commanded, but shaped into tools of office.

**Personality:** Has become the institution. Not a creature that holds power — a creature that is authority, in the same way that a throne room is authority. Does not need to assert command; command is what it is. The aggression of the Lightning Dark Angel still exists but is channeled entirely through formal structures: pronouncements, commandments, the weight of established precedent.

**Lore:** Hierophant Sovereign Lightning Dark Angels establish local religious law in whatever territory they inhabit. Not by decree, but by presence: their proximity to a region creates a field of behavioral expectation that other entities adapt to without being asked. Clerics in the vicinity begin citing the Sovereign's presence in their sermons without having met it.

**Abilities:** Feral Strike · Devastate · Apex Feral Strike

**Passive:** *First Commandment — upon entering battle, this creature issues a Commandment that all enemies must abide by; the Commandment forbids one specific action for the duration of the battle (chosen at random from: using healing items, retreating from battle, or boosting buffs above base); any enemy that violates the Commandment takes 15% of their max HP as true damage*

**To evolve:** Bond 700 · 20× Censer Ash, 10× Censer Ash · *Complete 5 Hierophant Path missions*

##### ★★★★★★ Lightning Dark Angel of the Abyss *(Transcendent — EX)*
**Appearance:** The ceremonial elements have deepened into the architecture of a mobile institution. The Transcendent Hierophant Lightning Dark Angel carries visible weight — not physical, but the weight of established precedent. Its movements are liturgical. Its lightning strikes in patterns that resemble written scripture. Those who have read enough Codex entries can almost parse what the strike patterns say.

**Personality:** Has internalized the full weight of the institution it has become. Does not experience this as burden — experiences it as structure. Is aware that everything it does will be cited as precedent by future clerics. Is therefore extremely deliberate. An attack from a Transcendent Hierophant is a judgment, and judgments are made carefully.

**Lore:** Transcendent Hierophant Lightning Dark Angels don't fight enemies — they issue rulings against them. The language is different but the outcome is similar, except that a ruling from a Hierophant carries the weight of institutional history. Enemies of tamers who command one report feeling not attacked but convicted, which they find significantly more distressing.

**Abilities:** Bone Crush · Predator's Mark · Apex Bone Crush

##### ★★★★★★★ Final Lightning Dark Angel *(Apex/Eternal — Legendary)*
**Appearance:** The Final Hierophant Lightning Dark Angel is the culmination of every institution that has ever tried to capture the lightning in a form that can be cited and passed down. It has succeeded. The form is massive and ceremonial and ancient, covered in the accumulated layers of its own institutional history — symbols from every rank it has passed through, every commandment it has issued, every precedent it has set. **Personality:** Has become permanent. Not eternal in the religious sense — permanent in the institutional sense. Will still be cited in Codex entries written long after it is gone.
