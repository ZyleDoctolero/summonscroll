## 🌍 Realm Ecology

The Void Frontier is the edge of the known world — a realm of floating asteroid cities, nebula-born
creatures, and starships made of crystallized light. Its monsters ride solar winds and navigate
by constellation maps tattooed on their bodies. They are explorers by nature, which means they are
also the creatures most likely to arrive somewhere completely unexpected and immediately adapt.

The Frontier produces the fastest-evolving monsters in any realm — not because they evolve
more easily, but because they seek out experiences that trigger evolution. A Frontier monster
that is not discovering something new considers itself failing at its purpose.

### Ecological Zones

| Zone | Description |

## 🎭 Tamer Bond System: Navigator

**Bond Method:** Exploration — discover 3 new Codex entries with the monster as companion. The discoveries must be new to the tamer.

Navigators are tamers who explore alongside their monsters. The bond is formed through discovery —
not through combat, prayer, or waiting, but through finding something neither of them has seen
before. The monster accompanies the tamer until they discover 3 new Codex entries together (entries
the tamer has never seen before). At that point, the monster considers the bond formed.

Navigators can flee any battle without penalty. At Master rank, they gain Stellar Chart — can see
the locations of all undiscovered areas on the current map. At Legend rank, they unlock Final
Horizon — once per week, teleport to any discovered location instantly, bypassing all obstacles.

### Path Descriptions

**Path A — Nova**
Nova Path — Becomes a living stellar explosion. Forms glow with contained nuclear energy, body temperatures measurable. Abilities focus on area destruction, blinding attacks, and radiant damage.

**Path B — Void Walker**
Void Walker Path — Learns to navigate between stars — literally, in the gaps. Forms become partially non-physical, moving through attacks. Abilities focus on phasing, portal generation, and repositioning the entire battlefield.

**Path C — Star Sovereign**
Star Sovereign Path — Achieves dominion over a constellation. Forms become vast and mapped, with star-lines visible across their bodies. Abilities focus on constellation buffs (activating star-patterns for different effects), gravity control, and cosmic authority.

## 🌟 Apex Quest: *The Last Horizon*

The Last Horizon activates when the Navigator has discovered every zone in all 12 realms and
found 50 hidden Codex entries. A star chart appears in the sky above the Frontier that only the
tamer can read. Following it leads to a location outside any mapped realm — a point in void space
between all realms simultaneously. There, the tamer's primary Stellar ally looks at the horizon
and decides what it wants to become. The World-Soul form reflects that decision.

## 🔮 World-Soul Form (★8 — Mythic)

A Void Frontier World-Soul monster exists at the leading edge of exploration — literally, it
always occupies the furthest point from where it started. When it is not moving, it is preparing

be flanked, surprised, or ambushed, and it deals 25% bonus damage to any enemy it has not
