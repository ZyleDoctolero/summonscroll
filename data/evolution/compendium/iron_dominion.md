## 🌍 Realm Ecology

The Iron Dominion is a continent-spanning factory-empire where nature has been entirely replaced
by industry. Smoke-stacks rise like mountains. Brass rivers run through engineered channels.
Every living surface has been measured, optimized, and assigned a production quota. The sky is
permanently overcast with productive smog that the realm's inhabitants have decided is a feature.

Its creatures are hybrids of beast and machine — whether by choice, necessity, or conquest. Some
embrace their mechanical additions with pride; others bear them as wounds. The Dominion does not
acknowledge the distinction. All that matters is output. Creatures that produce are supported.
Creatures that do not are repurposed.

Sources: Warhammer 40K Adeptus Mechanicus / Necrons, Neon Genesis Evangelion, Gurren Lagann,
Pacific Rim, Gundam, Armored Core VI, Full Metal Panic, Code Geass, Ghost in the Shell, classic
steampunk literature (Howl's Moving Castle, Mortal Engines, Fullmetal Alchemist).

## 🎭 Tamer Bond System: Engineer

**Bond Method:** Craft an item using the monster's unique material output. The monster decides if the result is worthy.

Engineers are tamers who prove their worth through utility — specifically, by crafting an item
using materials provided by the target monster's body (scales, steam exhaust, gear teeth). This
is not extraction; the monster chooses what to provide. The tamer must then use those materials
to make something the monster considers valuable. The monster decides if it counts.

Engineers can attach one gear upgrade per monster (passive equipment slot that stacks with normal
gear). At Master rank, they gain Schematics — can copy one ability from any defeated machine-type
monster and apply it to a party member once. At Legend rank, they unlock Grand Commission —
once per month, commission a unique weapon from the monster that carries its ability as an
innate property.

### Path Descriptions

**Path A — War Machine**
War Machine Path — Military optimization. Forms add armament layers — cannons, missile racks, blade arrays. Inspired by Warhammer's Daemon Engines and Pacific Rim Jaegers. Abilities focus on artillery, suppression fire, and tactical area control.

**Path B — Steam Titan**
Steam Titan Path — Industrial giant. Forms expand massively, powered by enormous boilers and pressure systems. Inspired by Victorian steampunk literature. Abilities focus on steam-powered AoE, pressure venting, and unstoppable momentum.

**Path C — Iron Sovereign**
Iron Sovereign Path — Transcends function and becomes principle. Forms achieve perfect mechanical elegance — complex but not complicated, powerful but not wasteful. Inspired by Armored Core's IBIS series and Evangelion's awakened forms. Abilities focus on efficiency multipliers, execution, and inevitable victory conditions.

## 🌟 Apex Quest: *The Grand Forge*

The Grand Forge is a 50-player cooperative raid. It activates for a server once the combined
Engineering output of all Iron Dominion tamers on the server reaches a threshold (measured by
items crafted). The raid requires players to assemble the First Machine — an ancient device
predating the Dominion itself — by contributing specific components. Completion for the
tamer who places the final piece triggers the World-Soul transformation.

##### ★★★★★ Boiler Knight Apprentice — Steam Titan Sovereign *(Sovereign — Elite)*
**Appearance:** The creature has grown. Not evolved — grown, in the way a boiler expands under sustained pressure. It stands three times its Champion height. Four additional pressure gauges have emerged across its torso, all reading in the red. A second boiler has budded off the original and fused to its back. Engineers who study it note that it does not appear to consider retreating a valid option that requires dismissal — it simply has never computed it as a possibility.

**Lore:** Seismometers in settlements adjacent to its territory register its footsteps at magnitude 1.2. Iron Dominion infrastructure crews pre-reinforce roads before it migrates. Factories in neighboring regions report their own boilers running 5% hotter in its presence — whether from resonance or inspiration, scholars cannot agree.

#### 🔱 IRON SOVEREIGN PATH

*Iron Sovereign Path — Transcends function and becomes principle. Forms achieve perfect mechanical elegance — complex but not complicated, powerful but not wasteful. Inspired by Armored Core's IBIS series and Evangelion's awakened forms. Abilities focus on efficiency multipliers, execution, and inevitable victory conditions.*

##### ★★★★★ Boiler Knight Apprentice — Iron Sovereign Sovereign *(Sovereign — Elite)*
**Appearance:** It has become smaller since the Exalted stage. Every redundant component has been removed. The boiler is now a compact, perfectly sealed core buried beneath three geometric plates — no exposed pipes, no visible heat venting, no wasted surface. Its silhouette is a clean, angular form that engineers describe as "the shape something would be if it had no ambition except to function perfectly." It moves without sound. Not quietly — without sound. The mechanisms are efficient enough that they produce no friction noise whatsoever.

**Personality:** Philosophical. It has achieved mechanical enlightenment: the understanding that every action has exactly one optimal expression, and wasteful action is not merely inefficient — it is a form of dishonesty. It does not converse. It responds, briefly, when addressed. It has said things that engineers have repeated to each other for years without fully understanding them. The most recorded quote: "Every extra word is a flaw."

**Lore:** Engineers pilgrim to observe an Iron Sovereign Boiler Knight Apprentice in the same spirit that scholars visit great libraries. The Iron Dominion's laws of efficiency — the Foundry Codex, specifically Articles 7 through 14 — were drafted by observing its movement patterns over a period of three years. The drafters noted that the Codex is not instructions on how to be efficient; it is a description of what this creature already does naturally.

**Abilities:** Perfect Strike · Efficiency Surge · Execution Protocol

**Passive:** *Zero Waste — all abilities cost 10% less energy; every ability that hits the optimal target (highest threat value) deals +20% bonus damage automatically*

**To evolve:** Bond 700 · 20× Boiler Shard, 10× Boiler Shard · *Complete 5 Iron Sovereign Path missions*

##### ★★★★★★ Boiler Knight Apprentice the Feared *(Transcendent — EX)*
**Appearance:** It has continued to shrink. The Transcendent Iron Sovereign is the same height as its Champion form — but where the Champion had mass, this has density. Each visible surface is a perfect geometric plane. Engineers who study it cannot find a single angle that serves no structural purpose, a single edge that exists for aesthetics, or a single component that does not perform multiple simultaneous functions. Its movement is so efficient that high-speed camera analysis shows its limbs completing motions before the joints have visibly begun moving. It looks still even when walking.

**Personality:** It wastes nothing, including words. It has been recorded speaking seventeen times since reaching this tier. All seventeen instances were single sentences. All seventeen were correct. It does not offer these sentences — it states them when the information is necessary, and then becomes silent again. Tamers report that its silence carries more weight than most creatures' speeches. Being in its presence for extended periods causes people to become more efficient in ways they cannot explain.

**Lore:** The Iron Dominion's engineering academies use recordings of its movement as the gold standard for mechanical efficiency. Not aspirationally — literally. Students are graded on how closely their designs approximate the motion ratios it demonstrates. The Transcendent form has never suffered damage in a recorded engagement. Not because it is invulnerable, but because its threat assessment is so accurate that it has never been in a position where taking damage was the optimal outcome.

**Abilities:** Inevitable Strike · Efficiency Multiplier · [Empowered] Perfect Strike · Geometric Execution

**Passive:** *Mechanical Enlightenment — upon entering battle, calculates the optimal victory condition and gains +15% to all relevant stats for that condition; if the calculation is correct (battle ends as predicted), all stats reset at +25% for the next battle*
