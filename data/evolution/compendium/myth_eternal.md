## 🌍 Realm Ecology

Myth Eternal exists at the boundary between reality and story — a realm where myths are not
about things that happened but things that are still happening, continuously, on a frequency
most beings cannot perceive. The first dragon still sleeps here, coiled around the world's axis.
The first gods still argue here about who created whom.

Every creature in Myth Eternal is a walking narrative. They cannot be merely defeated — stories
do not end when the protagonist loses once. They must be outlasted, or the tamer must become
part of their story in a way that changes its direction. Tamers who try to brute-force Myth
Eternal find the realm actively rewriting the encounter to maintain its narrative logic.

This realm contains creatures from ALL major mythology traditions — Greek, Norse, Egyptian,
Hindu/Vedic, Chinese, Japanese, Celtic, Mesopotamian, Mesoamerican, and Slavic — because
all myths share a single deep structure, and that structure lives here.

## 🎭 Tamer Bond System: Myth-Speaker

**Bond Method:** Tell a worthy story — write a narrative about the monster in the game's journal. The monster judges whether it is compelling enough.

Myth-Speakers are tamers whose stories are interesting enough to be acknowledged by mythological
entities. The bond process requires the tamer to tell a story — written in the game's journal
system — about the monster they wish to bond with. The monster reads it. If the narrative is
compelling, the bond forms. If it is boring, the monster ignores the tamer until they try again.

Myth-Speakers can rewrite one combat outcome per battle (literally: choose a different result for
one dice roll). At Master rank, they gain Narrative Sense — enemies with "story significance"
(bosses, named monsters) deal 30% less damage against the Myth-Speaker. At Legend rank, they
unlock The Story Continues — once per week, prevent any permanent consequence (death, item loss,
quest failure) by narrating an alternative outcome.

### Path Descriptions

**Path A — Titan Born**
Titan Born Path — Connects to the primordial giant lineage. Forms grow immense, heavy, mountain-like. Greek Titans, Norse Giants, Hindu Asuras, and Mesoamerican Colossus archetypes all converge here. Abilities focus on physical dominance, seismic events, and world-scale force.

**Path B — Myth Ascendant**
Myth Ascendant Path — The creature achieves mythological significance in its own right. Forms become story-dense — events happen around them that mirror classic mythological patterns. Abilities focus on narrative inevitability, fate manipulation, and the myth cycle (defeat once = stronger return).

**Path C — Origin God**
Origin God Path — The creature claims or reclaims divine status at the world's foundation. Forms become primordial, serene, operating outside normal physical logic. Abilities focus on creation events, absolute authority, and the ability to speak rules into being.

## 🌟 Apex Quest: *Before the First Word*

Before the First Word is a quest that begins when the Myth-Speaker has bonded with at least one
creature from each of the 10 mythology traditions in the realm. A blank page appears in their
journal — not blank, but written in a script that predates every known language. Decoding it
(using clues scattered across all 12 realms' Codex entries) reveals the First Story. Re-telling
it perfectly in the journal, in the correct language, triggers the World-Soul transformation.

## 🔮 World-Soul Form (★8 — Mythic)

A Myth Eternal World-Soul monster is the myth of itself made fully real. It exists simultaneously
in all timeframes — past, present, and the futures that were considered and rejected. Looking
at it long enough reveals different versions of itself at different moments. Its ★8 Passive,
"The Living Story," means it cannot be permanently defeated — if killed, it revives at the
next in-game dawn with 50% HP, because stories do not end. The only way to truly stop a
World-Soul Myth Eternal monster is to complete its story arc.

**Unlock Conditions:**
1. Complete *Before the First Word* story quest
2. Reach Myth-Speaker Legend rank
3. Bond of 5,000+ with the target monster
4. Obtain: **World Mythology Prime Stone (Server World Event — completing 'The First Story' collaborative quest)**
5. Participate in the server-wide World Event for Myth Eternal

Scholars who study the Origin God path report that the creature seems to predate its own myth — as if it existed before the story was told about it, and the story was built retroactively to explain something that was already simply true. Tamers bonded with one notice a slow shift in how others react to them: a baseline calm that wasn't there before, as if the bond has granted them a fraction of the creature's foundational quality.
