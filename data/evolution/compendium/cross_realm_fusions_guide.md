# SummonScroll — Cross-Realm Fusion System
## The Forbidden Alchemy

Cross-Realm Fusion is a late-game mechanic that allows tamers to bypass traditional evolutionary paths by merging two ★5 (Sovereign) or higher monsters from different realms. 

This process is **permanent**. The original monsters are lost, their data merged into a brand new entity that possesses traits of both realms. There are exactly **20 documented fusion families** in the game.

### How Fusion Works:
1. **Bond Requirement:** Both monsters must have max bond (700+) with the tamer.
2. **Catalyst:** A specific, ultra-rare catalyst item is required to stabilize the cross-realm energy.
3. **The Altar:** Fusions can only be performed at the *Convergence Altar*, a hidden location found at the geographical center of the world map.
4. **Result:** The resulting monster starts at ★6 (Transcendent) and has access to a unique skill tree combining elements of both parent realms.

### Plague Grove
**Realms:** Blighted Expanse × Wild Frontier

> A creature born from the convergence of death and wilderness — a lich-druid that tends a garden of poisonous undead flora.

#### Requirements to Fuse
- **Parent A:** Any ★★★★+ Blighted Expanse monster
- **Parent B:** Any ★★★★+ Wild Frontier monster
- **Catalyst Item:** Cross-Realm Convergence Stone (World Event Drop)
- **Special Condition:** Both monsters must have 800+ Bond

#### The Evolution Chain
**★ Echo Plague Grove**
- **Tier:** Hatchling | **Rarity:** Common
- **Role:** Attacker
- **Appearance:** A small, delicate form of Plague Grove. Its body is simple — barely touched by the Death element — but its eyes already carry an ancient awareness far beyond its size.
- **Passive:** *Tidal Flow — HP regenerates 2% per turn*

**★★ First-Form Plague Grove**
- **Tier:** Awakened | **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Role:** Attacker
- **Appearance:** The Death element begins to manifest: faint glowing sigils trace along its hide, and it moves with deliberate intent. Its form has grown noticeably, and other creatures instinctively give it space.
- **Passive:** *Data Overlock — +25% SPD when HP above 75%*

**★★★ Plague Grove**
- **Tier:** Champion | **Rarity:** Rare
- **Role:** Attacker
- **Appearance:** The Champion form — the creature as the world knows it. Every feature reflects its Blighted Expanse × Wild Frontier origin: the palette, the silhouette, the elemental signature of Death radiating freely. Battle-tested and undeniable.
- **Passive:** *Ancient Ward — reduces incoming magical damage by 15%*

**★★★★ Exalted Plague Grove**
- **Tier:** Exalted | **Rarity:** Epic
- **Role:** Attacker
- **Appearance:** At the Exalted threshold, the creature restructures. Blighted Expanse × Wild Frontier-specific motifs deepen dramatically. The path chosen — Convergent — begins to reshape its silhouette, and a visible aura of power surrounds it at all times.
- **Passive:** *Berserker Soul — ATK rises 3% for each 1% HP lost*
