# SummonScroll — The 12 Tamer Classes
## Grand Master Guide

In SummonScroll, a player does not simply catch monsters. To unlock a monster's true potential, the player must adopt a **Tamer Class** corresponding to that monster's realm. 

A player can hold multiple class titles simultaneously, but can only have **one active Class** during combat. Switching active classes requires resting at a sanctuary.

---

## 📈 The Class Progression System

Every class ranks up from **Initiate** to **Legend**. 
Ranking up requires a combination of high bond levels with realm-specific monsters, completing realm-specific story quests, and utilizing the class's unique mechanics.

* **Initiate:** The bond is formed. You can command the monster.
* **Apprentice:** The base class passive is unlocked for combat.
* **Adept:** A powerful exploration/utility passive is unlocked.
* **Master:** A secondary combat passive or systemic buff is unlocked.
* **Legend:** The ultimate class ability is unlocked (usually limited to once per day/week due to immense power).

## Grave Keeper (Realm: Blighted Expanse)

> *"Those who understand that an ending is just a transition."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Death-and-rebirth oath ceremony (The Rite of Passing)

Grave Keepers are the most patient tamers in the known world. They do not command through force, but through shared understanding of mortality. A Grave Keeper is never rushed, never desperate, and never fears losing a battle.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Completes the Rite of Passing. Monster allows the tamer to give commands.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Free Revive).
- **[Adept]** Gains 'Death-Speak' — can communicate with any undead entity in the world.
- **[Master]** Undead monsters treat the tamer as a superior; no random encounters in graveyards.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Grave Command' — once per week, resurrect an enemy monster to fight for you for one battle.

## Pack-Mate (Realm: Wild Frontier)

> *"Those who run with the hunt."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Trial of the Hunt + Shared Meal

Pack-Mates abandon the concept of 'owner' and 'pet'. They are part of the hierarchy. If a Pack-Mate shows weakness, their own monsters might challenge them for leadership. The bond is visceral, raw, and completely honest.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Shares a meal with a beast. Gains basic command respect.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Pack Tactics).
- **[Adept]** Can read territorial markers; navigate Wild Frontier without triggering encounters.
- **[Master]** Beasts in the wild will occasionally bring the tamer rare crafting materials.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Pack Call' — once per day, summon a local wild monster as a temporary ally.

## Prelate (Realm: Divine Threshold)

> *"Those whose attention is a form of prayer."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Shared prayer + 3 selfless acts (no-reward quests)

Prelates are orderly, disciplined, and politically powerful. They navigate the celestial hierarchy, trading favors with archangels and cherubim. A high-ranking Prelate can stop a war just by arriving on the battlefield.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Recognized by a celestial entity. Gains access to basic temples.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Divine Grace).
- **[Adept]** Can enter Restricted Temples. Healing items are 50% more effective.
- **[Master]** Divine creatures will never ambush the party. Undead enemies suffer -10% ATK.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Divine Mandate' — once per battle, force all enemies to attempt to flee.

## Watcher (Realm: The Outer Dark)

> *"Those who are comfortable with the silence."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Wait in silence for 30 in-game days until chosen.

Watchers do not choose their monsters. They are chosen. They are often quiet, unnerving individuals who seem to be looking at things just slightly behind you. They do not panic, because the Void has already shown them how the battle ends.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Acknowledged by the Void. Gains basic command.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Dread Presence).
- **[Adept]** Cosmic Sight — see hidden traps, invisible enemies, and true stats of opponents.
- **[Master]** Immune to all mind-altering status effects (fear, confusion, madness).
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'The Watched' — once per battle, a fatal blow against your monster simply misses.

## Scribe (Realm: Ancient Vaults)

> *"Those who understand that a fact is a weapon."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Submit an original piece of knowledge to the Codex.

Scribes treat battles as data collection. They are scholars first, combatants second. An Ancient Vaults monster bonded to a Scribe acts as a research assistant, analyzing enemy attacks and calculating optimal counter-measures in real time.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Granted library access. Monster acknowledges intellect.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Analytic Eye).
- **[Adept]** Vault Access — can check drop rates and evolution paths for any encountered monster.
- **[Master]** Spellbooks and scrolls cost 50% less mana to use. EXP gain increased by 15%.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Living Tome' — once per week, summon any monster you have fully researched in the Codex.

## Champion (Realm: Chaos Wastes)

> *"Those who speak the language of absolute violence."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Fight the monster. Win, or lose impressively.

Champions are warlords. There is no diplomacy in their bond, only mutual respect forged in blood. A Chaos monster will only follow a tamer it believes could kill it, or at least die trying. It is the most aggressive, high-risk bond in the game.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Survives the trial by combat. Monster submits to strength.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Blood Rage).
- **[Adept]** War Title — Wastes creatures respect you. No ambush attacks in Chaos regions.
- **[Master]** All physical attacks have a 10% chance to cause 'Bleed' status.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Last Stand' — a dying monster instead enters Berserk (1 HP, +50% ATK) for 3 turns.

## Confidant (Realm: Haunted Veil)

> *"Those who trade in secrets and regret."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Reveal a genuine, costly personal secret to the monster.

Confidants share a terrifying intimacy with their monsters. Because the bond is based on radical honesty, a Confidant cannot lie to their monster. In return, the monster protects the tamer's mind from the horrors of the Veil.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Secret accepted. Monster binds itself to the truth.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Clear Mind).
- **[Adept]** Can speak the native language of the Veil. Understand all ghost/undead NPC dialogue.
- **[Master]** Enemies afflicted with Fear take 25% more damage from all sources.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Dark Mirror' — manifest your own deepest fear as an autonomous ally for one battle.

## Operator (Realm: Digital Nexus)

> *"Those who compile intention into action."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Complete one Nexus objective together flawlessly.

Operators view battles as puzzles to be solved algorithmically. They interface via Soul Terminals, transmitting thoughts as code. The bond is clean, efficient, and lacks emotional volatility — a perfect machine synthesis.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Handshake protocol established. Basic commands accepted.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Macro Execution).
- **[Adept]** System Access — see exact enemy HP numbers, buff durations, and next intended action.
- **[Master]** Cooldowns on all monster abilities are reduced by 1 turn.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Root Protocol' — once per day, execute a monster's Ultimate Ability instantly with no cooldown or cost.

## Tide-Speaker (Realm: Elder Realm)

> *"Those who remember what the water remembers."*

**The Bond Ritual:** 30 in-game days of passive companionship (no combat).

Tide-Speakers operate on geological time. They do not rush. Bonding with an Elder creature means accepting memories of extinct civilizations and primordial oceans. They are the anchors of any team, impossible to move or break.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Patience proven. The ocean acknowledges the tamer.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Deep Fortitude).
- **[Adept]** Memory Gifts — occasionally receive visions revealing hidden paths, items, or lore.
- **[Master]** Water-based movement is 50% faster. Cannot drown.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Tide's Call' — once per battle, summon a wave that staggers enemies and cleanses all ally debuffs.

## Navigator (Realm: Void Frontier)

> *"Those who only look at the horizon."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Discover 3 new Codex entries alongside the monster.

Navigators are explorers. They bond with stellar entities through the shared thrill of discovery. A Navigator who stays in a city too long will find their monsters growing restless and depressed. They belong in the unknown.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Curiosity aligned. The journey begins.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Tactical Retreat).
- **[Adept]** Stellar Chart — reveals all undiscovered points of interest on the current map.
- **[Master]** Movement speed increased by 20% in all zones. Immune to environmental damage.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Final Horizon' — once per week, instantly teleport to any discovered location in the game.

## Myth-Speaker (Realm: Myth Eternal)

> *"Those who understand that history is just a draft."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Write a compelling story about the monster in the journal.

Myth-Speakers bend reality through narrative. They recognize that the world is made of tropes, arcs, and dramatic tension. By aligning themselves with a mythological entity, they gain the power to act as the author of the battle.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** The story is accepted into the mythos.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Narrative Edit).
- **[Adept]** Narrative Sense — take 30% less damage from 'named' or 'boss' enemies.
- **[Master]** Can read the 'fate' of NPCs, predicting quest outcomes before starting them.
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'The Story Continues' — once per week, prevent a permanent consequence (death, lost item, failed quest).

## Engineer (Realm: Iron Dominion)

> *"Those who forge intention into leverage."*

**The Bond Ritual:** Craft an item using materials freely given by the monster.

Engineers are pragmatic builders. They do not care about magic or destiny; they care about torque, output, and efficiency. Bonding with a machine-beast is a mutual upgrade protocol. The tamer improves the monster, the monster provides the power.

### Class Evolution Tree
- **[Initiate]** Craftsmanship respected. Integration begins.
- **[Apprentice]** Unlocks the base passive (Hardware Upgrade).
- **[Adept]** Schematics — copy one ability from a defeated machine enemy and apply it to a party member.
- **[Master]** Crafting costs reduced by 30%. Created items have a 10% chance to be 'Masterwork' (+20% stats).
- **[Legend]** Unlocks 'Grand Commission' — once per month, commission a unique weapon bearing a monster's innate passive.
