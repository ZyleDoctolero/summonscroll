## World Ecology

The Chaos Wastes are a continent-spanning battlefield where the ground itself mutates. Lava cracks run in patterns that look like wounds, and the sky is perpetually ash-red. Creatures here grow stronger only through combat — the weak are consumed by the strong, and evolution is measured in scars. Tamers must defeat their monster in combat before it will acknowledge them.

---

## Tamer Bond System

Chaos tamers are called Champions. Bond is formed through combat — the tamer must fight the monster and either win or earn its respect through bravery in defeat.
