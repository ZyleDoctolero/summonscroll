## World Ecology

The Ancient Vaults are a labyrinthine library-dungeon where every wall is a book and every book is alive. Creatures here evolved to guard, copy, and devour knowledge. Some are made of compressed text; others are golems animated by the first words ever written. Tamers must pass written trials to earn the attention of Vault creatures.
