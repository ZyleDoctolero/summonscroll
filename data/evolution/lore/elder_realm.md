## World Ecology

The Elder Realm lies beneath all other realms — an infinite ocean that predates land itself. Its creatures are the oldest living things in the world. Coral cities the size of kingdoms line the ocean floor. The creatures here do not evolve quickly; they grow wise instead, accumulating centuries of memory in their shells and scales. Tamers must bring gifts of knowledge, not combat.
