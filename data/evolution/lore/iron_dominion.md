## World Ecology

The Iron Dominion is a continent-spanning factory-empire where nature has been entirely replaced by industry. Smoke-stacks rise like mountains, brass rivers run through engineered channels, and every creature has been partially mechanized — whether by choice or conquest. Its monsters are hybrids of beast and machine, and they respect only efficiency and output.
